Easy2say / 好說

Requires iOS 26 or iPadOS 26. Grant microphone access, pick two languages, then tap Start. Conversation mode uses two on-device transcribers; tap a half of the screen to claim who is speaking if the guess is wrong.

The first time you choose Taigi, Breeze-ASR-26 may take several minutes to specialize on this device. Later launches reuse that cache. Output is labelled as Mandarin-character transcription, not Taibun.

Feedback: au@civic.ai · source: github.com/audreyt/v2s
